Code of Conduct

Northstar Labs · Effective October 19, 2026 · This page will be updated after legal review.

Northstar is an AI guide. This page says, in plain words, how Northstar behaves, how it keeps you safe, and how we ask you to use it.

How Northstar behaves

  • It's an AI, not a person. Northstar can make mistakes, so check anything important.
  • It's honest. It never makes up businesses, ratings, results or photos. Examples on our website are labeled as examples.
  • You decide. Northstar suggests; you make the final call. It never books, pays or sends anything without you.
  • It's not a professional. Northstar is not a doctor, therapist, lawyer or financial advisor, and AI is not a substitute for licensed professional advice. For health, legal or money decisions, talk to a licensed professional.
  • It's fair. It never rates a neighborhood as safe or unsafe, good or bad.
  • Your memory is yours. No ads, and we don't sell your information. See our Privacy page.
  • Built on Claude. Northstar's answers are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Anthropic's own safety rules apply too.

How Northstar keeps you safe

These are Northstar's safety steps (our safety protocol):

  • Crisis help comes first. If someone mentions wanting to die, suicide, self-harm, abuse or not being safe, Northstar responds with care and points them to a real person right away: in the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline); in immediate danger, call 911; outside the US, the local emergency number. The app shows Call 988 and Text 988 buttons.
  • Two layers. Northstar's own code checks for crisis words before the AI answers, and the AI follows the same crisis rules. Either one is enough to bring up help.
  • No harmful details. Northstar never gives methods or instructions for self-harm.
  • Good Listener is there to listen. It isn't therapy and can't diagnose or treat. Before it's switched on, a safety notice explains this, and it can't be turned on without seeing that notice first.
  • On this website, the live Discovery and Chat phones follow the same crisis rules.
  • Young people. Northstar isn't intended for children under 13, and it may not be suitable for some minors.
  • We keep improving these steps and review them regularly. If you see Northstar get something wrong, please tell us.

How we ask you to use Northstar

  • Be kind. Don't use Northstar to harass, threaten or harm anyone.
  • Nothing illegal, and nothing meant to deceive, impersonate or scam someone.
  • Don't try to trick Northstar into unsafe behavior or around its limits.
  • Don't share other people's private information without their OK.
  • Anthropic's Usage Policy also applies, because Northstar runs on Claude.

We may limit or stop access for anyone who misuses Northstar.
